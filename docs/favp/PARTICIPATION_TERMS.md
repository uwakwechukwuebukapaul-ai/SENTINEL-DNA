# FAVP Participation Terms

**Status:** Draft for legal and security review
**Version:** 1.0
**Parties:** Sentinel DNA program operator and `[Participant/legal entity]`

These terms are an operational framework, not a completed contract. Legal,
privacy, security, and procurement owners must adapt and approve them for the
participant, jurisdiction, compensation model, and intended disclosures.

## 1. Program scope

The participant may perform only the synthetic scenarios, through the access
path, tenant, tools, and dates stated in the approved participation record.
The program owner may narrow, pause, or stop the scope at any time for safety,
security, evidence, availability, or conduct reasons.

## 2. Synthetic-data boundary

The participant must use synthetic data only. The participant must not upload,
paste, expose, infer from, or otherwise introduce production data, customer
data, personal data, regulated data, live credentials, secrets, or incident
material. An accidental submission must be reported immediately; the
participant must not attempt to conceal or independently remediate it.

## 3. Access and acceptable use

Access is personal, time-limited, least-privileged, and revocable. The
participant must not share access, create additional accounts, use another
person's session, bypass controls, probe unrelated tenants, alter audit records,
or attempt actions outside the approved scenarios.

## 4. Human decision authority

The participant remains responsible for the final analyst conclusion. System,
automation, or AI output is advisory only. The participant must record whether
the advisory material was accepted, challenged, or rejected and why.

## 5. Evidence and feedback

The participant agrees to provide structured feedback and non-secret evidence
references sufficient for review. The program may record scenario outcomes,
timings, control observations, evidence provenance, and de-identified feedback
within the approved purpose and retention period.

## 6. Confidentiality

The participant must protect non-public program materials, access details,
findings, and other confidential information and disclose them only to
authorized recipients. Any separate NDA controls in case of conflict. The
participant must report suspected disclosure promptly through the approved
security channel.

## 7. Security incident and stop condition

The participant must stop work and notify the program/security owner upon
observing unexpected tenant access, credential or secret exposure, origin
failure, audit/provenance inconsistency, unauthorized capability, or any other
material control concern. The program owner may revoke access and preserve
non-secret evidence for investigation.

## 8. Withdrawal and revocation

The participant may withdraw through the approved channel. The operator may
terminate participation for security, scope, conduct, availability, legal, or
program reasons. On withdrawal or termination, access will be revoked and any
required session invalidation verified.

## 9. Feedback and intellectual property

The parties must specify, before participation:

- ownership of participant-created materials;
- whether feedback is licensed or assigned;
- whether de-identified quotations may be used;
- whether any public disclosure requires separate written approval;
- treatment of pre-existing materials and third-party materials.

No public statement should identify a participant, employer, customer, or
finding without the required written approval.

## 10. Compensation and expenses

Compensation, honoraria, expenses, tax treatment, invoicing, and payment timing
must be stated in a separate approved schedule or here:

`[Complete before acceptance]`

## 11. Privacy and retention

The program owner must state the categories of participant information
collected, purpose, access recipients, retention period, deletion process,
cross-border handling, and participant rights in the applicable privacy notice
or agreement.

## 12. Legal review items

Counsel must review confidentiality, data protection, export controls,
employment/contractor classification, liability, indemnity, warranties,
intellectual property, governing law, dispute process, accessibility, and
sector-specific obligations before execution.
