## What participation involves

Subject to application review and written agreement, participants will:

- complete a short security and workflow orientation;
- work through a defined set of synthetic investigation scenarios;
- record independent reasoning, evidence references, uncertainty, and final
  conclusions;
- assess whether system-generated material is useful, incomplete, or wrong;
- report access, provenance, usability, and workflow issues;
- complete a closeout and access-revocation check.

The analyst remains the final decision maker. Any system or AI output is
advisory and may be rejected.

## Boundaries

- The program uses synthetic data only.
- No production system, customer tenant, customer record, or live incident is
  in scope.
- Access is limited to an approved person, tenant, scenario set, and time
  window.
- Participants must not attempt to bypass security, tenant, origin, audit, or
  evidence controls.
- Do not send passwords, tokens, cookies, private keys, or session material in
  email, chat, forms, screenshots, or feedback.
- Participation does not authorize public statements, customer commitments,
  resale, or independent security claims.
