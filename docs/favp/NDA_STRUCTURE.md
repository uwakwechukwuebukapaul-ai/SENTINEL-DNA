# FAVP NDA Structure

**Status:** Counsel review outline; not an executed agreement
**Purpose:** identify the provisions required for a participant confidentiality
agreement before access or non-public disclosure

This outline deliberately leaves legal choices open. Counsel should produce
the operative agreement for the participant's jurisdiction, entity structure,
data flows, term, and disclosure model.

## 1. Parties and authority

- legal names and addresses of the parties;
- authority of each signatory;
- affiliates or subcontractors, if any;
- relationship to the FAVP participation terms.

## 2. Purpose and permitted use

Define the limited purpose: evaluating Sentinel DNA workflow and evidence
handling in a controlled program. Confidential information may be used only
for that purpose and not for competitive analysis outside the agreed scope,
publicity, customer representations, or unrelated product development.

## 3. Confidential information

Cover, as applicable:

- non-public product, architecture, workflow, and security information;
- staging access details and control observations;
- program materials, scenarios, findings, and evidence references;
- participant feedback and non-public business information;
- the existence and terms of the engagement where appropriate.

The agreement should state how oral, visual, written, electronic, and marked or
unmarked information is treated.

## 4. Exclusions

Address information that is demonstrably:

- public without breach;
- already lawfully known;
- independently developed without use of protected information;
- lawfully received from a third party without confidentiality duty;
- required to be disclosed by law, subject to the notice process.

## 5. Recipient obligations

Require reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards;
need-to-know access; no unauthorized copying; no credential or session
handling; no reverse engineering beyond the written program scope; and prompt
incident notification.

## 6. Program-specific data boundary

State that FAVP exercises use synthetic data only. Prohibit customer,
production, personal, regulated, credential, cookie, token, private-key, and
browser-session material unless a separate written scope and legal/security
review expressly changes the boundary.

## 7. Compelled disclosure

Define notice, cooperation, protective-order efforts, and the minimum required
disclosure process.

## 8. Security incident process

Identify notification channels, response times, preservation duties, contact
roles, containment cooperation, and the relationship to access revocation and
program stop conditions.

## 9. Return, deletion, and retention

Specify return/deletion of confidential materials, permitted archival copies,
legal holds, evidence-custody records, deletion confirmation, and the
retention period approved by privacy/security owners.

## 10. Ownership and feedback

Clarify ownership of pre-existing material, participant-created work,
structured feedback, de-identified insights, and any license or assignment.
No publication or quotation should occur without the agreed approval process.

## 11. Term and survival

Set the confidentiality term, trade-secret treatment, survival periods,
termination rights, and effect of program withdrawal.

## 12. No authority or representation

State that participation does not grant authority to act for Sentinel DNA,
make customer commitments, issue security statements, or represent program
findings as independent assurance.

## 13. Legal boilerplate

Counsel should address governing law, venue, notices, assignment, relationship
of parties, severability, waiver, entire agreement, amendments, order of
precedence, electronic signatures, accessibility, and language.

## Approval checklist

- [ ] Legal review complete for participant and jurisdiction.
- [ ] Privacy review complete for participant data and retention.
- [ ] Security review complete for scope and incident handling.
- [ ] Participation terms and NDA precedence agreed.
- [ ] Signature/acceptance evidence recorded before access.
