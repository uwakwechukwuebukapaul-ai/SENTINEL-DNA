# Design Partner Recruitment and Outreach Guide

## Outreach objective

Move a qualified FAVP participant from interest to a documented design-partner
conversation. Outreach must describe the actual scope, use synthetic data, and
avoid implying results, product performance, or a purchase decision.

## Target segments

- FAVP participants with relevant security-analysis experience;
- SOC managers and security operations leaders who can sponsor a bounded
  evaluation;
- security engineering, detection, response, and GRC leaders who own evidence
  quality or workflow improvement.

## LinkedIn outreach strategy

1. Use a permission-based, personalized message referencing a genuine FAVP
   interaction or approved professional context.
2. Do not include confidential findings, architecture, customer names, incident
   details, or unverified claims in public messages.
3. Ask for a short discovery conversation, not an immediate purchase.
4. Move substantive material to an approved private channel after consent.
5. Record opt-out requests and do not continue outreach after opt-out.
6. Use only approved brand, privacy, and legal language.

## Discovery call structure

1. Confirm the participant's role and reason for interest.
2. Describe the problem and proposed scope without outcome claims.
3. Confirm synthetic-data, access, confidentiality, and human-authority
   boundaries.
4. Understand the current workflow, decision owner, and evidence needs.
5. Discuss organizational readiness and timing.
6. Explain selection, agreement, onboarding, and exit steps.
7. Record open questions, conflicts, and next action.

## Screening questionnaire

- What workflow and decision would you want to examine?
- Who owns the operational and security decision?
- Can the evaluation use synthetic or approved sanitized data only?
- Which analyst roles would participate, and who approves their access?
- What evidence and provenance must be available for internal review?
- What access boundaries and stop conditions are required?
- What time can the organization commit during the evaluation window?
- Which legal, privacy, procurement, or security reviews are required?
- What would make the engagement useful even if no purchase follows?
- What conflicts, competitive relationships, or publication restrictions exist?

## Interview guide

### Analyst interview

- Walk through a recent evidence-driven investigation at a high level without
  sharing confidential data.
- How do you distinguish facts, assumptions, and missing evidence?
- How do you challenge a recommendation that conflicts with your judgment?
- What makes an audit trail trustworthy?
- What would make you stop using a workflow?

### Security leader interview

- What risk and control owner would sponsor the evaluation?
- What evidence is required for security and procurement review?
- What tenant, access, retention, and audit requirements apply?
- What would be a valid commercial decision process after validation?
- What claims or references would require additional approval?
