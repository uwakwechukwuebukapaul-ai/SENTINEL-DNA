# Design Partner Commercial Conversion Framework

## Commercial principle

Design partnership is a validation relationship, not a free proof of purchase
or a guarantee of product fit. Commercial progression requires evidence,
stakeholder alignment, security review, and an explicit buyer decision.

The core Sentinel DNA subscription remains the primary commercial model.

- **Sentinel DNA subscription:** core platform and operating workflow.
- **Sentinel DNA Trust Fabric:** premium expansion layer for organizations that
  require deeper trust, access, evidence, or governance capabilities.
- **DNA Proof:** evidence and provenance infrastructure supporting reviewable
  decisions and audit workflows.
- **Trust Exchange:** future enterprise/API ecosystem direction; not part of
  the initial design-partner offer unless separately approved.

No NFT or tokenized ownership concept is part of this model.

## Conversion path

```text
Design Partner
  -> Validation Complete
  -> Paid Pilot Proposal
  -> Enterprise Pilot
  -> Subscription Customer
```

Each transition is a decision gate, not an automatic funnel state.

## Stage definitions and triggers

### Design Partner

Entry requires approved selection, agreement, security scope, onboarding, and
an owned validation question.

### Validation Complete

Triggers:

- agreed scenarios and feedback cadence completed or formally closed;
- evidence/provenance reviewed;
- control issues and limitations documented;
- partner and Sentinel DNA owners agree on the decision record;
- business problem, buyer, and next-step hypothesis are documented.

This state does not mean the product is ready for every organization.

### Paid Pilot Proposal

Triggers:

- a bounded business problem remains meaningful to the prospective buyer;
- the buyer identifies decision criteria and budget owner;
- security, privacy, procurement, and data scope are sufficiently defined;
- proposed pilot outcomes are measurable and evidence-based;
- paid scope, duration, support, price basis, and exit criteria are drafted.

### Enterprise Pilot

Requires executed commercial terms, customer security approval, technical
onboarding, named owners, agreed support, data processing terms where needed,
and a separate production or customer-environment decision.

### Subscription Customer

Requires a completed pilot review, customer acceptance, subscription agreement,
renewal owner, support model, and documented expansion decision. Do not infer
conversion from an expression of interest.

## Pricing discussion framework

Discuss:

- number and type of users/analysts;
- tenant or business-unit scope;
- modules and service boundaries;
- deployment and support model;
- pilot duration and success/exit criteria;
- security, privacy, procurement, and legal requirements;
- implementation or enablement work;
- subscription term and renewal model;
- premium Trust Fabric expansion needs;
- evidence/provenance requirements supported by DNA Proof.

Use approved price cards or a documented pricing exception. Do not invent a
price, discount, ROI, savings percentage, or revenue forecast in program
materials.

## Paid pilot package structure

- executive problem statement and scope;
- named customer sponsor, analyst owner, security owner, and Sentinel DNA
  owners;
- approved environment, tenant, dataset, and data-processing boundary;
- validation scenarios and evidence requirements;
- onboarding and support plan;
- security, audit, access, and revocation controls;
- measurable decision criteria and baseline method;
- fee, billing, term, and change-control terms;
- exit, rollback, data deletion, and renewal review;
- final report and customer acceptance process.

## Customer success process

1. Confirm desired business and security outcomes.
2. Establish baseline, scope, owners, and evidence plan.
3. Complete technical/security onboarding.
4. Run agreed pilot cadence and executive checkpoints.
5. Review evidence, findings, objections, and open risks.
6. Make a human customer decision: continue, narrow, pause, or stop.
7. If continuing, complete subscription and success-plan handoff.

## Renewal indicators

Consider renewal only from evidence such as:

- recurring approved usage by the intended analyst group;
- evidence quality and provenance meeting customer criteria;
- resolved or accepted security findings;
- active business owner and budget path;
- support and governance workload understood;
- customer-confirmed value in the defined scope;
- no unresolved access, data, or audit blocker.

Renewal indicators are not guarantees and should be recorded with dates,
denominators, and customer references.
