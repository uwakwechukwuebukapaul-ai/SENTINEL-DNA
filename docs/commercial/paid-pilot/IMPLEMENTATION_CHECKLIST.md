# Paid Pilot Conversion Implementation Checklist

Use one checklist per candidate. An unchecked required item blocks the next gate.

## Program setup

- `[ ]` Program owner, commercial owner, security owner, customer-success owner, and legal reviewer assigned.
- `[ ]` Approved price card, order-form template, security schedule, and data-processing terms available.
- `[ ]` Design Partner and FAVP records linked to the candidate.
- `[ ]` CRM stages and required evidence fields configured.
- `[ ]` Customer Proof approval register configured.

## Qualification and proposal

- `[ ]` Validated workflow and limitations documented.
- `[ ]` Customer sponsor, analyst lead, security owner, and budget/procurement contacts identified.
- `[ ]` Package, users, environments, integrations, evidence volume, support, duration, and success criteria selected.
- `[ ]` Pricing and any services approved.
- `[ ]` Stop conditions and closeout decision owner agreed.

## Legal and security

- `[ ]` NDA/confidentiality coverage confirmed.
- `[ ]` Pilot agreement and order form approved and signed.
- `[ ]` Data classes, retention, deletion, residency, and subprocessors reviewed.
- `[ ]` Tenant isolation, RBAC, audit, evidence custody, and incident paths approved.
- `[ ]` Human decision authority and AI limitations included.

## Customer onboarding

- `[ ]` Named identities and MFA/identity process verified.
- `[ ]` Least-privilege RBAC configured and reviewed.
- `[ ]` Approved synthetic/sanitized dataset loaded.
- `[ ]` Audit logging and evidence custody verified.
- `[ ]` Tenant-isolation and unauthorized-access checks passed.
- `[ ]` Analyst orientation complete.
- `[ ]` Readiness decision recorded.

## Pilot execution

- `[ ]` Kickoff completed and baseline recorded.
- `[ ]` Weekly operating cadence active.
- `[ ]` Investigations, evidence, blockers, and feedback recorded.
- `[ ]` AI recommendation and human decision reviews completed.
- `[ ]` Scope changes approved before implementation.
- `[ ]` Security exceptions, if any, are documented and approved; unresolved critical exceptions block execution.

## Closeout and conversion

- `[ ]` TRUST, USAGE, EVIDENCE, and PAYMENT scorecards complete or marked not measured.
- `[ ]` Evidence index and limitations reviewed with customer.
- `[ ]` Customer decision recorded: stop, extend, repeat, continue, or expand.
- `[ ]` Subscription proposal uses approved scope and price.
- `[ ]` Procurement and renewal owners assigned.
- `[ ]` Access revoked or renewed under a new approved agreement.
- `[ ]` Evidence retention/export/deletion disposition recorded.

## Proof and governance

- `[ ]` No claim or quote used without source and written approval.
- `[ ]` Anonymization/privacy review complete where applicable.
- `[ ]` Public/customer-facing artifact versioned with expiry/review date.
- `[ ]` KPI values have source, denominator, period, owner, and quality status.
