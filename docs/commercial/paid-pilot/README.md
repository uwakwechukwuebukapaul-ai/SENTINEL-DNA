# Sentinel DNA Design Partner → Paid Pilot Conversion System

Status: operating framework for review and controlled use. It is not an offer, order form, security certification, or customer result.

## Purpose

This package turns a completed Design Partner engagement into a governed, evidence-based paid pilot. It defines commercial stages, customer controls, measurement, proof governance, and expansion conversations without presuming that a pilot will succeed or convert.

The package complements:

- [Founding Analyst Validation Program](../../favp/FAVP_PROGRAM_CHARTER.md)
- [Design Partner Program](../../design-partners/PROGRAM_CHARTER.md)

## Operating sequence

1. Complete Design Partner validation and document what was and was not measured.
2. Confirm paid-pilot readiness with the customer sponsor, security owner, procurement owner, and analyst users.
3. Agree scope, fees, data handling, success criteria, and stop conditions in an approved order form.
4. Complete tenant, RBAC, audit, evidence-custody, and analyst onboarding gates.
5. Execute the pilot with human analysts retaining final decision authority.
6. Review evidence and scorecards with the customer; record a continue, expand, or stop decision.
7. If justified, negotiate an annual subscription and separately approved enterprise expansion.

No production access, customer claim, or revenue forecast is implied by this documentation.

## Guardrails

- Synthetic or sanitized data is the default for validation; production data requires explicit customer and security approval.
- Sentinel DNA recommendations are advisory. Analysts and customer operators remain accountable for decisions and actions.
- Evidence provenance, tenant isolation, auditability, and fail-closed controls remain mandatory at every stage.
- Credentials, cookies, tokens, browser sessions, and authentication secrets are not stored in this package.
- Pricing, customer names, outcomes, and conversion rates are placeholders until approved and evidenced.
