# Customer Proof System

## Purpose

This process creates factual, permissioned customer proof without fabricating customers, results, quotes, or revenue. A pilot can produce useful internal evidence without producing publishable proof.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Internal pilot record with methodology, scope, denominator, and limitations.
2. Anonymized result approved for the stated audience.
3. Attributed analyst quote approved by the speaker and customer.
4. Customer-approved case study with scope, period, method, and limitations.
5. Public reference, only when separately approved.

Higher visibility requires more review; it does not turn an inconclusive result into a success claim.

## Proof workflow

1. Identify a candidate observation from the evidence index.
2. Confirm it is factual, reproducible, and within contracted scope.
3. Remove or redact confidential, personal, tenant, security-sensitive, and identifying information.
4. Prepare the draft with method, cohort, period, denominator, limitations, and evidence reference.
5. Obtain analyst consent for quotes and customer written approval for any attribution or publication.
6. Obtain Sentinel DNA security, legal, and communications approval.
7. Store the approval, version, audience, expiry/review date, and permitted channels.
8. Revalidate before reuse; retract or correct when the underlying scope or evidence changes.

## Permitted formats

### Testimonials and analyst quotes

Use exact approved wording, speaker role, organization attribution, date, and approval reference. Paraphrases must be labeled as paraphrases and approved by the speaker where attribution remains possible.

### Case studies

Include customer-approved problem statement, defined workflow, method, evidence, measured observations, limitations, customer decision, and date. Avoid implying that one customer’s experience generalizes to all environments.

### Anonymized results

Use a minimum-identification review appropriate to the customer and threat model. Do not include unique workflow details, timestamps, tenant identifiers, rare configurations, or combined facts that could re-identify the customer. Keep the internal source reference separate from the public artifact.

## Prohibited claims

- Fabricated or unverified customers, logos, quotes, results, percentages, savings, revenue, or security outcomes.
- “Certified”, “guaranteed”, “autonomous”, “eliminates risk”, or equivalent claims without an approved basis.
- Cherry-picked cases presented as representative.
- Claims that an AI recommendation was a human decision or that evidence was complete when limitations exist.

| Field | Value |

| Expiry or review date | `[Date]` |
| Owner | `[Named owner]` |

## Correction and withdrawal

Any customer request, evidence correction, security concern, or scope change triggers review. The owner must suspend distribution, correct or withdraw the artifact, notify affected recipients where appropriate, and retain the decision history.
