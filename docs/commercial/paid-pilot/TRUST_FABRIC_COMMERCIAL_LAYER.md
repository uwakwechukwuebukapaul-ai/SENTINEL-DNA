# Trust Fabric Commercial Layer

## Commercial architecture

The commercial model keeps the Sentinel DNA subscription as the core revenue product. The following capabilities are expansion concepts, not guaranteed features, customer outcomes, or automatic entitlements:

| Capability | Commercial role | Evidence required before expansion |
|---|---|---|
| Sentinel DNA Trust Fabric | Premium enterprise layer for governance, trust controls, cross-workflow evidence operations, and approved organizational integration | Named customer need, scoped architecture, security review, approved packaging |
| DNA Proof | Evidence and provenance infrastructure supporting integrity, custody, review, and decision traceability | Defined evidence requirement, retention/custody design, customer acceptance |
| Trust Exchange | Future enterprise/API ecosystem capability for approved trust and evidence exchange | Product readiness, API/security review, ecosystem governance, commercial approval |

Trust Exchange is a future capability unless an approved product and security review states otherwise. No NFT concepts are part of this model.

## Value hypotheses to validate

Conversations may test whether customers need stronger evidence governance, cross-team review, controlled sharing, provenance operations, or integration governance. These are hypotheses until the customer defines a problem, a measurement method, and an approved scope.

## Expansion triggers

An expansion proposal may be considered when:

- The customer has an active or approved Sentinel DNA subscription.
- A recurring requirement exceeds the core scope and is documented.
- The customer accepts the data, tenancy, access, audit, and operational model.
- A value hypothesis can be evaluated without unsupported claims.
- Product, security, legal, and commercial owners approve the proposed capability.

## Security boundaries

Premium packaging must not weaken tenant isolation, least privilege, audit logging, evidence custody, certified origin enforcement, human decision authority, or fail-closed behavior. Cross-tenant or external exchange, if ever introduced, requires explicit authorization, data minimization, recipient controls, revocation, and audit evidence.

## Customer conversation prompts

- Which evidence or governance requirement is recurring and costly to manage?
- Which teams or workflows need controlled visibility?
- Which data must remain tenant-bound or customer-controlled?
- What would a safe, measurable expansion test look like?
- Which security and procurement owners must approve it?

## Packaging and roadmap gates

Do not sell a capability based only on a roadmap statement. Record status as `available`, `pilot-approved`, `planned`, or `not available`, with owner and review date. A premium proposal must identify the exact service boundary, limits, support, data handling, and exit path.
