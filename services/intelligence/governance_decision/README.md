# Governance Decision Intelligence
This package transforms existing compliance, monitoring, reporting, posture, exposure, incident, executive-risk, operations, and command-center signals into a deterministic, tenant-isolated advisory decision queue.

It does not replace compliance, GRC, risk management, the Evidence Engine, or Command Center. It does not execute remediation, modify controls, make autonomous business decisions, certify compliance, or trigger external actions. Every decision is advisory and requires human review. Signals retain source and evidence references; priorities are explainable weights, not objective business decisions. The repository can be replaced by SQLite/PostgreSQL without changing service contracts.
