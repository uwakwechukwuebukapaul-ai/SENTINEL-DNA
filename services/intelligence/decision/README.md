# Investigation decision intelligence

Decision intelligence is the deterministic post-investigation layer that turns collected evidence, reasoning, risk, and confidence into a bounded SOC verdict. Reasoning explains what the evidence suggests; decision intelligence chooses an operational verdict and recommended next action. It does not replace orchestration, AI runtime, evidence reasoning, or historical memory.

Future integrations may add analyst approval workflows, autonomous response recommendations, SOAR playbook selection, risk-based prioritization, and executive reporting without changing this decision contract.
